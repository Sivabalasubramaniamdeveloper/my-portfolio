CORE HIGHLIGHTS:
1. AI Voice & Speech Task Extraction: Real-time speech transcription to SQLite with Gemini 2.5 Flash entity parsing.
2. Parallel Price Comparison Scraper: Amazon, Flipkart, Meesho real-time price and deal difference calculations.
3. Geo-Bound City News: Strict city boundary filtering for 8 major Indian cities with editorial photo extraction.
4. AI Email Assistant: Professional tone formulation and direct SMTP dispatch.
5. Biometric Private Vault: Client-side AES-256 encrypted storage for sensitive keys and credentials.
6. HLS Video OTT Player: Adaptive multi-bitrate streaming with gesture seeking and variable playback speeds.
7. Admin Dashboard: Real-time user metrics, support ticket resolution, and system health.

REVIEWER / EVALUATOR INSTRUCTIONS:

- Windows: Run the setup executable or unzip portable build.
- macOS: Unzip and drag to Applications. If Gatekeeper prompts, right-click and select 'Open'.
